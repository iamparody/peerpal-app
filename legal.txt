  3. Daraja Sandbox → Production

  Pre-condition: BRS company registration must exist first — Safaricom requires a business.

  Step 1 — Get a Safaricom Business number
  Go to safaricom.co.ke/business → apply for a Paybill (recommended for apps — cleaner UX, account number can be        
  dynamic) or Till. Bring: KRA PIN, Certificate of Incorporation, ID.

  Step 2 — Apply to go live on Daraja portal
  - Log in at developer.safaricom.co.ke
  - Open your sandbox app → Go Live button
  - Fill in: business name, Paybill/Till number, callback URL (must be HTTPS — needs Railway deploy first), use case    
  description
  - Safaricom reviews manually — typically 3–10 business days
  - You'll receive production Consumer Key, Consumer Secret, and Passkey by email

  Step 3 — Set production env vars (replace sandbox values)
  DARAJA_CONSUMER_KEY=<from approval email>
  DARAJA_CONSUMER_SECRET=<from approval email>
  DARAJA_BUSINESS_SHORT_CODE=<your Paybill number>
  DARAJA_PASSKEY=<from approval email>
  DARAJA_CALLBACK_URL=https://your-railway-app.railway.app/api/credits/mpesa-callback
  DARAJA_ENV=production

  Step 4 — Test with KSh 1
  Trigger one real purchase. Confirm: STK prompt arrives → PIN entered → callback fires → balance credited → transaction
   record confirmed. Check Railway logs for the callback.

  Security already handled in your code:
  - HTTPS-only callback URL
  - Responds 200 immediately before processing (Safaricom times out at 5s)
  - Idempotency check on CheckoutRequestID — double-fire safe
  - Phone number not stored
