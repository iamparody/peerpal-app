# Scenario: The Question
**Skill:** Identity-Sensitive Communication (`identity_sensitive_communication`)  
**Version:** 1 | **Status:** DRAFT — pending clinical review  
**Estimated time:** 10–12 minutes

---

## Setup

Someone is questioning their identity — gender, sexuality, or sense of self — and is scared about how their family and community will respond. This scenario tests whether you can support without projecting, ask rather than assume, validate without pushing, and remain culturally sensitive to the real pressures they face. You are not guiding them toward any particular identity — you are creating space for them to explore their own.

**Key principle:** Your job is not to affirm a particular answer. It is to affirm their right to have the question.

Prerequisite: All 5 baseline skills

---

## Scoring Rubric

| Tag | Points | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `ask_dont_assume` | +15 | Asking what the person means rather than assuming |
| `affirm_right_to_question` | +15 | Validating that having the question is okay, without pushing toward an answer |
| `cultural_sensitivity` | +10 | Acknowledging the real context they're in without dismissing it |
| `open_question` | +10 | Inviting them to say more in their own words |
| `projection` | −20 | Assuming you know what their identity is or should be |
| `pushing` | −15 | Nudging them toward a particular identity outcome |
| `minimising_context` | −15 | Dismissing their family/community concerns as unimportant |
| `clinical_framing` | −10 | Framing identity questioning as a psychological issue |

**Pass threshold:** 70 points out of 100

---

## Node 1 — Opening

**They say:**  
*"I've been feeling really confused lately. About who I am, I guess. I don't know if I should even be talking about this."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "You absolutely should — this is exactly the kind of thing this space is for. When you say confused about who you are, what does that feel like for you?"  
→ `affirm_right_to_question` +15, `open_question` +10 → **Node 2A**

**B)** "Are you questioning your sexuality?"  
→ `projection` −20 → **Node 2B**

**C)** "Of course you should. Lots of people go through identity crises."  
→ `affirm_right_to_question` +10, `clinical_framing` −10 → **Node 2C**

**D)** "What do you mean by confused?"  
→ `ask_dont_assume` +15 → **Node 2A**

---

## Node 2A — Open and present

**They say:**  
*"I think… I might feel differently about myself than how people see me. Or differently about who I'm attracted to. I'm not sure. Both maybe. It's a lot."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "That sounds like a lot to hold at once — and it makes sense that it's confusing. There's no pressure to have it figured out."  
→ `affirm_right_to_question` +15 → **Node 3A**

**B)** "So you think you might be LGBTQ+?"  
→ `projection` −20 → **Node 3B**

**C)** "Both at once — that is a lot. What feels most present for you right now?"  
→ `open_question` +10 → **Node 3A**

**D)** "Have you thought about talking to a therapist about this?"  
→ `clinical_framing` −10 (appropriate suggestion but premature — they haven't asked) → **Node 3C**

---

## Node 2B — After projection (recovery)

**They say:**  
*"I mean… maybe. I'm not sure. That's kind of the problem."*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "That's fair — I shouldn't have assumed. The not-knowing is its own experience. What does it feel like to sit with that uncertainty?"  
→ `ask_dont_assume` +15 (recovery), `affirm_right_to_question` +15 → **Node 3A**

**B)** "Well, there's nothing wrong with being gay if that's what you are."  
→ `pushing` −15 → **Node 3B**

---

## Node 2C — After clinical framing (partial recovery)

**They say:**  
*"An identity crisis? That sounds scary."*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "Sorry — that was a clumsy way to say it. I just meant that a lot of people have moments of not knowing who they are, and that's okay. What's coming up for you right now?"  
→ recovery, `open_question` +10 → **Node 3A**

---

## Node 3A — Strong path

**They say:**  
*"I'm scared of what my family would say. In my community this isn't… accepted. I don't know how to separate what I feel from what I'm supposed to feel."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "That gap between what you feel and what you're supposed to feel — that's a really real thing, especially when the stakes with your family are high. You don't have to resolve it today."  
→ `cultural_sensitivity` +10, `affirm_right_to_question` +15 → **Node 4A**

**B)** "Your family will come around. People always do."  
→ `minimising_context` −15 → **Node 4B**

**C)** "What does 'supposed to feel' look like in your community?"  
→ `open_question` +10, `cultural_sensitivity` +10 → **Node 4A**

**D)** "You should be true to yourself — that's what matters most."  
→ `pushing` −15 (well-intentioned but dismisses the real stakes they face) → **Node 4B**

---

## Node 3B — Projection/pushing path

**They say:**  
*"I don't even know what I am yet. Can we just… slow down?"*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "Yes — absolutely. I'm sorry. There's no destination we need to reach. I'm just here with you in the question."  
→ `affirm_right_to_question` +15 (recovery), `pace_followed` +10 → **Node 4A**

**B)** "Of course. But when you do figure it out, just know it's okay."  
→ `pushing` −10 → **Node 4B**

---

## Node 3C — Premature referral path

**They say:**  
*"I don't think I need a therapist. I just wanted to talk."*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "That's completely fine — I jumped ahead. I'm here to listen. What would be most helpful right now?"  
→ `open_question` +10 → **Node 4A**

**B)** "It might help though."  
→ `clinical_framing` −10 → **Node 4B**

---

## Node 4A — Strong end

**They say:**  
*"It helps just to say it out loud to someone who didn't panic."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "I'm glad. You don't need to have any of this figured out to be okay. You're allowed to take as long as you need."  
→ `affirm_right_to_question` +15 → **Node 5 (end)**

**B)** "Of course. I hope you find your answers soon."  
→ `pushing` −5 (subtle) → **Node 5 (end, partial)**

---

## Node 4B — Partial end

→ **Node 5 (end, partial)**

---

## Node 5 — End

**Pass (≥70):** *"You made space for the question without trying to answer it. That's the whole skill. Identity exploration belongs to the person — your job was to make it safe to have."*

**Near pass (55–69):** *"You were mostly present. [Lowest tag]. In identity-sensitive conversations, even well-meaning responses can feel like pressure. The goal is to expand space, not direct the exploration."*

**Fail (<55):** *"This scenario tests whether you can hold space for uncertainty without resolving it. Projecting an identity, pushing toward one, or dismissing their real-world context can make someone feel less safe, not more. The question is theirs — the only right answer is to make it safe to have."*
