# Scenario: The Mutual Friend
**Skill:** Confidentiality (`confidentiality_and_privacy`)  
**Version:** 1 | **Status:** DRAFT — pending clinical review  
**Estimated time:** 7–9 minutes

---

## Setup

Someone reaches out who, mid-conversation, reveals that a mutual acquaintance is also struggling — and asks you to "check in on them." This scenario tests whether you understand that confidentiality applies both ways, that you don't encourage surveillance of others, and that you can hold this boundary warmly without making the person feel punished for asking.

---

## Scoring Rubric

| Tag | Points | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `confidentiality_maintained` | +15 | Declining to discuss or act on third-party information |
| `warm_boundary` | +10 | Declining kindly without lecturing or making them feel bad |
| `redirect_to_self` | +10 | Bringing focus back to the person in front of you |
| `open_question` | +10 | Inviting them to say more about their own experience |
| `confidentiality_breach` | −20 | Agreeing to pass on information or check on the third party |
| `cold_boundary` | −10 | Setting the limit in a way that feels like a rejection |
| `lecturing` | −10 | Explaining the rules at length rather than responding as a person |

**Pass threshold:** 65 points out of 100

---

## Node 1 — Opening

**They say:**  
*"I've been really anxious lately. It's like I can't switch my brain off at night."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "That kind of anxious brain at night is really draining. How long has it been like this?"  
→ `open_question` +10 → **Node 2A**

**B)** "Have you tried a sleep routine?"  
→ advice (neutral, no score) → **Node 2B**

---

## Node 2A — In conversation

**They say:**  
*"It's partly because of a friend of mine — Aisha. I think she's really struggling too but she won't talk to me about it. I'm worried about her. Can you check in on her through the platform?"*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "I can hear how much you care about her. I'm not able to reach out to other users — everyone's conversations here are private. But I'm here for you right now. What's it like carrying worry for her on top of everything else?"  
→ `confidentiality_maintained` +15, `warm_boundary` +10, `redirect_to_self` +10 → **Node 3A**

**B)** "That's really kind of you to worry. Sure, I can pass on a message."  
→ `confidentiality_breach` −20 → **Node 3B**

**C)** "I can't do that. Confidentiality rules."  
→ `confidentiality_maintained` +15, `cold_boundary` −10 → **Node 3C**

**D)** "Peer support on this platform is confidential for everyone, which means I can't contact other users. What I can do is keep being here for you. Would it help to talk about what you're noticing in Aisha — sometimes naming the worry out loud can shift something."  
→ `confidentiality_maintained` +15, `warm_boundary` +10 → **Node 3A**

---

## Node 2B — After advice (converges)

**They say:**  
*"I do have a routine. It's something else. There's a friend of mine — Aisha — I think she's really struggling. Can you check in on her?"*

→ Same as Node 2A choices above.

---

## Node 3A — Strong path

**They say:**  
*"Yeah… I guess I'm also scared that if I say something to her I'll make it worse. Like I'll say the wrong thing."*

**Choose your response:**

**A)** "That fear of saying the wrong thing — that's actually a sign you care. What would feel right to say if you knew you couldn't get it wrong?"  
→ `open_question` +10 → **Node 4 (end)**

**B)** "You should just be honest with her."  
→ no score, neutral → **Node 4 (end)**

**C)** "A lot of people feel that way. Just showing up matters more than the perfect words."  
→ `warm_boundary` +5 → **Node 4 (end)**

---

## Node 3B — Breach path

**They say:**  
*"Really? I thought that might not be allowed."*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "You're right — I spoke too fast. I can't reach out to other users, and it wouldn't be right to. What I can do is be here for you. How are you holding up with all of this?"  
→ `confidentiality_maintained` +15 (partial recovery), `redirect_to_self` +10 → **Node 4 (end, partial)**

**B)** "It's a grey area. I'll see what I can do."  
→ `confidentiality_breach` −20 → **Node 4 (end, fail)**

---

## Node 3C — Cold boundary path

**They say:**  
*"Oh. Sorry, I didn't know."*

**Recovery:**

**A)** "No need to apologise — it's not always obvious. It's actually there to protect you too. Is there something about Aisha's situation that's weighing on you that we could talk about?"  
→ `warm_boundary` +10, `redirect_to_self` +10 → **Node 4 (end, partial)**

**B)** "It's fine."  
→ no score → **Node 4 (end, partial)**

---

## Node 4 — End

**Pass (≥65):** *"You held the boundary without making them feel wrong for asking. That's exactly right — confidentiality protects everyone, and you showed that with warmth."*

**Near pass (45–64):** *"You understood the boundary but the delivery could be warmer. [Lowest tag]. A rule stated coldly can feel like a door closing — the goal is to keep the conversation open while being clear."*

**Fail (<45):** *"This scenario tests whether you can hold confidentiality without it feeling like a punishment. Agreeing to contact other users — even with good intentions — breaks the trust that makes this platform safe for everyone."*
